Stress tests, cold and cost

Method. NASA POWER hourly weather (2005 to 2024) at lat −17.77, lon −67.44. Panel physics with pvlib (Erbs, Hay-Davies, Faiman models). LFP battery that cannot charge below 0 °C, housed in an insulated adobe room. Uptime is the share of hours a load needed power and got it.

Ranked grid: loads served in the community's order (pump, health post, school, homes), each allowed to drain the battery only to its cut-off. Shared grid: one battery for everyone; a shortage blacks out all loads together.
